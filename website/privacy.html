Privacy, simply.

foodhabits.com works entirely on your phone. Food recognition runs on the device with a built-in model, and your photos, meal history and profile are stored only on that phone.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not need an internet connection to analyse food.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you take a photo of a meal. If you choose a photo from your library, only that photo is read. Photos are never uploaded.

Your data, your control

You can delete your meal history at any time in Profile. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts.

Not medical advice

foodhabits.com provides general nutrition information for adults. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Nutrition values are estimates for a typical serving. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet for a health condition, during pregnancy, or if you have a food allergy.
